Quiet until useful

Look at the badge.
Go back to studying.

NeverLate stays silent in your toolbar until you need it. It updates your badge with your pending count, shows countdown tooltips on hover, and lets you check work off like Apple Reminders.

01

At a glance. Your Chrome badge shows pending deliverable count; hover to see the next deadline and countdown string.

02

One-click calendar export. Send any deadline to Google Calendar or Outlook 365, or export all active deadlines into Apple Calendar with 2-hour alarms.

03

Apple Reminders checkmarks. Finished an assignment early? Click Done to strike it through. Alarms for completed work are silenced immediately.

04

Offline manual deadlines. Add study groups, viva exams, or personal deliverables with custom classroom locations that survive Moodle logouts.

Built for students

Private means private.

NeverLate has no developer backend, no Firebase, and no database tracking what you submit. It runs entirely inside your browser.

01

Zero developer backend.

No external telemetry, cloud database, or analytics trackers. Network requests go only to your university's Moodle portal.

100% Client-Side
02

Ephemeral session key.

The Moodle sesskey is held only transiently in memory for internal AJAX calls. Passwords and capability secrets are never stored.

Memory-Only Sesskey
03

Server protection.

Multi-tiered throttling (10s manual, 45s auto attempt throttle, 120m cache cooldown) and exponential backoff protect LMS servers from strain.

Get NeverLate

15 seconds to install.
Then look up.

Requires Google Chrome, Brave, Arc, or Edge. Once loaded, open Plaksha LMS once to sign in. The extension automatically syncs and starts tracking.

Terminal / Setup
1 Download NeverLate-Plaksha.zip and unzip it.
2 Open Chrome and visit chrome://extensions in the address bar.
3 Turn on the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner.
4 Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
5 Open lms.plaksha.edu.in. You're done!
